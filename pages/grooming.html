For Pet Parents

What to Expect During Their Visit

We want your pet's visit to be the highlight of their week. Here is what you should know to prepare for their spa day:

Vaccination Requirements

For the safety of all pets, we require up-to-date Rabies, DHPP, and Bordetella vaccinations for dogs, and FVRCP for cats. Proof must be submitted prior to check-in.

Check-In & Intake Check

Please arrive 10 minutes early. We will do a quick 7-point health check of your pet's skin, coat, and posture to confirm there are no underlying conditions before starting.

Stress-Free Pick Up

We will send you a text message 15 minutes before your pet is ready. We encourage prompt pick-up to ensure they don't get anxious staying in the lobby.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a session take?

Most sessions range from 60 to 120 minutes depending on breed size and selected package.

What if my pet is anxious?

Our groomers are trained in low-stress handling techniques. Please mention any anxiety in the booking notes and we will ensure a calm environment.

Do you groom cats too?

Yes! We offer specialized feline grooming sessions in a separate, quiet area away from dogs.
